Dates: 2026-04-05 to 2026-04-06 (2 days)
Group: 2 adults + 2 kids (ages: 3, 4)

Origin: Liverpool, UK
Transport modes: train

## Travel options (origin → destination)
- Take a direct train from Liverpool Lime Street to Blackpool North - approximately 1 hour 30 minutes. Trains are family-friendly, with space for strollers and onboard restrooms.
- Consider off-peak travel hours for a more relaxed journey with young kids, usually mid-morning or early afternoon trains.
- Reserve seats if possible for added comfort and guaranteed seating, especially helpful with toddlers.

## Nearby places
- **Stanley Park** (Parks/Playgrounds) - Best for: [3, 4]
  - https://www.blackpool.gov.uk/Residents/Parks-and-community-facilities/Parks,-playgrounds-and-community-facilities/parks-and-sites/stanley-park.aspx
  - Stanley Park features stunning gardens and a children's play area designed for ages 2 to 8, including an animal farm themed play area. It offers modern playground equipment in a spacious, safe environment ideal for young children.
- **Blackpool Zoo** (Animals/Zoos/Aquariums) - Best for: [3, 4]

  - https://www.lovetovisit.com/uk-attractions/the-blackpool-tower-circus

## Day-by-day plan
### Day 1 (2026-04-05)
- Morning: Arrive in Blackpool by train from Liverpool. Settle into your chosen Airbnb family home. After unpacking, take a short walk to Stanley Park for a gentle morning play in the children's area and gardens, perfect for ages 3 and 4.
- Midday break: Return to the Airbnb for a lunch break with homemade sandwiches and fresh fruit. Allow toddlers a rest or nap time to recharge in a quiet, comfortable space.
- Afternoon: Visit Blackpool Zoo in the afternoon, where kids can enjoy seeing a variety of animals and engaging in safe, educational exhibits suitable for young children.
- Evening: Have a calm evening meal in the Airbnb using the kitchen. Then head out for a relaxed early evening stroll along the promenade to enjoy the sea air.
- Rain backup: If raining, spend the afternoon at Planet Kaos indoor play area, which is perfect for toddlers and offers plenty of active but safe play options indoors.
- Distances: Train Liverpool-Lime Street to Blackpool North ~1h 30m; Airbnb to Stanley Park ~10 minutes walk; Stanley Park to Blackpool Zoo ~5 minutes by car or taxi; Zoo back to Airbnb ~10 minutes by car or taxi; Evening promenade near Airbnb.

### Day 2 (2026-04-06)
- Morning: Start your day with breakfast at the Airbnb. Then visit the Blackpool Tower Circus for a fun family-friendly performance ideal for young children.
- Midday break: Return to the Airbnb to relax and have a lunch of simple pasta or soup prepared in the kitchen. Provide quiet downtime or naps for kids.
- Afternoon: Go to Stanley Park again or visit a nearby park like Anchorsholme Park for more outdoor playtime if weather permits. Alternatively, explore Blackpool's indoor Sandcastle Waterpark if the weather is cooler or wet.
- Evening: Enjoy a quiet evening at the Airbnb. Do some light games or storytelling to wind down and prepare for the journey home.
- Rain backup: On a rainy day, spend the afternoon at the Sandcastle Waterpark for indoor water fun in a safe and toddler-friendly environment.
- Distances: Airbnb to Blackpool Tower Circus ~15 minutes walk or short taxi ride; Circus back to Airbnb ~15 minutes; Airbnb to Stanley Park or Anchorsholme Park ~10-15 minutes by car; Waterpark alternative ~10 minutes by car.

## Travel tips
- Book train tickets early and reserve seats if possible for more comfortable travel with toddlers.
- Pack snacks and entertainment for the train journey to keep kids happy.
- Plan activities with plenty of breaks and downtime for kids’ naps and rest.
- Choose accommodations with kitchen facilities to prepare meals for young children easily.
- Bring layers and be prepared for unpredictable spring weather in the UK.
- Use local taxis or short car rides to minimize long walks or complicated transport with young kids.
